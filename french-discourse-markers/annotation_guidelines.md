# Provisional annotation guidelines

These labels are intentionally broad and are only a starting point. Read the whole sentence and its context; do not assign a label from the marker alone.

## consequence
Use when the segment containing the marker presents a result, consequence, conclusion, or practical outcome of preceding information.

## contrast
Use when the marker connects propositions that are presented as opposing, unexpected, or in tension.

## reformulation
Use when the speaker/writer corrects, clarifies, specifies, or replaces an earlier understanding or formulation.

## Important cautions
- A marker can have more than one discourse function in different contexts.
- Some examples may require the previous or following sentence to interpret correctly.
- If the function cannot be decided from the available context, mark the example `uncertain` in a future dataset rather than guessing.
- Record the annotator, source, document ID, and surrounding context when building a research dataset.
- Do not treat these provisional labels as a complete linguistic taxonomy.
