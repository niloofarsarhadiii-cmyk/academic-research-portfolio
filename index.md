

### Research Portfolio | Linguistics · Computational Linguistics · NLP

I am a multilingual researcher with an academic background in French literature, French Studies, and language education. My interests are increasingly focused on computational linguistics, particularly the relationship between linguistic structure, discourse, and computational language understanding.

My academic work has led me to questions about how meaning is constructed beyond the sentence level, how discourse relations are interpreted through context, and how language-specific features affect computational analysis.

I am particularly interested in computational discourse analysis, computational pragmatics, multilingual natural language processing, and language technologies for under-resourced languages.
